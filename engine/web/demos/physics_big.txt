# Big physics demo. Press Play. Four set pieces on one floor:
#  1. Bowling: a heavy ball rolls down a ramp into ten revolved pins.
#  2. Dominoes: a ball rolls down a small ramp and sets off a chain of fourteen.
#  3. Brick wall: a very heavy ball rolls down a ramp into a wall of twenty bricks.
#  4. Funnel: eight marbles drop into a static funnel and fall through its hole.
# Drag any prop to lift it and let go to throw it. Stop puts everything back.
