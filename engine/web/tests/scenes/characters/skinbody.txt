# SKIN BODY: the whole body as ONE closed surface from a skeleton of named joints (poly.skin = Blender's Skin modifier, the B-Mesh method), 1.75 m, matched to body.txt and head.txt.
# Each joint is `name x y z rx rz`: a position and the half extents of the cross-section across and along its bone. Joint heights come from the standard stature fractions the body audit
# uses (crotch 0.470, navel 0.600, elbow 0.630, shoulder 0.818, wrist 0.485, knee 0.285 of stature H = 1.75). The surface STOPS at an end joint, so the crown, toes and fingertips are joints.
# The left side is written, the right side mirrored by hand below (the `symmetry` setting only keeps quads from crossing the mirror plane). Fingers hang off a chain of knuckles, because
# several thin bones leaving one joint collide. Subdivided twice for a smooth surface; shaping and detail come after.
# Metres, +y up, facing +z.
