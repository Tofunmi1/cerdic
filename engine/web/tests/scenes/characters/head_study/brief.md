# Head study: measured semi-realistic game character head

Create a readable human head for a 1.72 m character. Preserve a calm, neutral expression and forward gaze. The model must read from front, three-quarter, and side views, with the eye line, nose, mouth, jaw, and skull proportions measured from the evaluated mesh.

The proportions in `spec.json` are the accepted target for this study, not universal anatomy rules. The audit tolerances are frozen while the model is edited. `world.txt` is the editable artifact; do not edit this brief, the spec, the audit, or fixed shots to make a failed result pass.

This is the first vertical slice of a larger character workflow. It audits head geometry and eyes. It does not claim a complete rigged or animated game character; expression poses, hair, ears, neck/body integration, and game export checks remain later slices.
