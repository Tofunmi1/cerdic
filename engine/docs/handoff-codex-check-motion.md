## Why

The owner's goal (2026-10-25): an LLM makes characters and animation itself. A model that writes a walk cannot see it. `ve_measure::motion` turns a sampled clip into numbers; this node samples the clip of the parts a model names and reports the two commonest faults in the tool reply, with a fix: a foot that skates while it should be planted, and a loop that pops at its seam.

## What to build

`check.motion@1(parts..., feet="", clip="", duration=1.0, fps=30, speed=0, slide_max=0.1, severity="warning")`:
- Inputs: a variadic list of at least one solid (the parts whose motion is sampled; `Arity::Variadic { min: 1 }`). Output `PortType::Check`, value `Value::nothing()`; the shapes are untouched.
- Params: `feet` string (comma separated **output names** of parts to measure for foot sliding, default empty: none); `clip` string (the clip to play, empty is the main clip); `duration` seconds 0.1 to 30 (default 1); `fps` int 5 to 120 (default 30; at most 3,600 frames); `speed` metres per second the character travels along +x while the clip plays in place (-20 to 20, default 0; added to every sampled world position as `x += speed * t`); `slide_max` metres per second of foot speed allowed while planted (0 to 5, default 0.1); `severity` `"warning"` | `"error"` (error fails the evaluation with the first finding). Read numbers with `Param::as_f64` (whole numbers arrive as integers in saved documents).
- Sampling (after the whole evaluation, over the finished parts): for each frame `t = i / fps` for `i` in `0..frames` (`frames = round(duration * fps)`, at least 2), a part's world transform is its own tracks of `clip` (and only those; a track from another clip is not played) composed in the listed order, then its parent's (`Part.parent` is the parent part's output name; follow the chain up to `ve_modeling::hierarchy::MAX_DEPTH`; a parent outside the evaluation is the root). **Compose exactly as the runtime does** (`ve-runtime/src/animation/part_1.rs` `pose_by`): per track in order, `position` adds a translation, `rotation` (Euler degrees, order YXZ) and `scale` act about the track's `pivot`, `matrix = step * matrix`; sample a track with `Track::sample(t)`, honouring `looped` as `Track::sample` does for a time past the last key (read it).
- **Foot slide:** for each name in `feet` (each must be an output name of a part in the evaluation, else a typed error naming it): the foot point is the lowest vertex (smallest y) of the part's rest mesh; its world position per frame is that point through the part's world transform, plus `(speed * t, 0, 0)`. Call `ve_measure::motion::foot_slide(points, 1/fps, 0.02)`. When `mean_speed > slide_max` push a warning with code `motion.foot_slide`, at the foot part's node, message such as `"the foot \"leg_a\" slides at 0.35 m/s on average (0.80 at most) while planted in clip \"walk\"; at a body speed of 1.4 m/s it should stand still in the world"` (give the numbers to 2 decimals and the clip name) and a suggestion such as `"shorten the swing, or make it match the travel speed: a planted foot moves back at the speed the body moves forward"`.
- **Loop seam:** the tracked points are the centres (bounding box centre of the rest mesh) of every listed part, each through its world transform per frame, **without** the travel term; call `loop_seam`. When not `seamless()` push `motion.loop_seam` at the first listed part, message such as `"clip \"walk\" does not loop cleanly: its last frame is 0.97 m from its first (a step is 0.03 m)"`, suggestion `"make the last key equal the first and set loop=true on every track of the clip"`.
- **No tracks:** when no listed part (nor any ancestor of one) has a track in `clip`, push one warning `motion.no_tracks` naming the clip (`"no part has an animation track in clip \"gallop\""`) and skip the measurements. (A listed foot with tracks only on an ancestor is fine.)
- A `ve_measure` refusal (`MeasureError`) becomes a clear typed diagnostic, never a panic. Findings are `EvaluationWarning`s with severity "warning"; with "error" the first finding fails the evaluation as a `Fail` with the same code.
- Operator doc (one paragraph the model reads): what it checks, that `feet` takes part output names, that `speed` is the travel speed of an in-place clip, and a one-line example.
